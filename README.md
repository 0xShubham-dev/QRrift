QRift — Offline QR Text Transfer

Bridge devices. Not servers.

QRift is a lightweight QR-based text transfer tool designed to help you move text between devices quickly and privately.

Generate a QR code from a long string, code snippet, URL, or clipboard item, then scan it using another device. No account, no cloud sync, and no server-side text storage.

Features:

- Text to QR: Convert text and long strings into QR codes.
- QR to text: Scan a QR code and retrieve its contents.
- Developer-friendly: Share code snippets, debugging information, URLs, and configuration strings.
- Lightweight: A simple interface designed to do one job well.
- Privacy-focused: Your text should remain on your device.
- Offline-capable: Designed to work without an internet connection once the required application files and dependencies are available locally.
- No account required: Open the app and get started.

How It Works:

1. Open QRift on your device.
2. Enter or paste the text you want to transfer.
3. Generate a QR code.
4. Scan it using another device's QR scanner.
5. Retrieve the encoded text on the receiving device.

For transferring data to a computer, use a QR reader that can display or copy the decoded contents.

Privacy

QRift is designed for local text processing without accounts, cloud synchronization, or server-side storage of the text being transferred.

Offline operation depends on the app's implementation and the availability of its required dependencies.

Avoid encoding passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive information into QR codes that could be seen or photographed by others.
